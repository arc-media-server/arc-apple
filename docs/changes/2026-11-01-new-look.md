# The new look: Liquid Glass, in step with the Android app

## What changed

The owner asked for Apple's current design language across the app, with their reference
screenshots as the look to aim for, and the Android TV app (itself modelled on Apple TV) as the
sibling to match (1 November).

- **Glass everywhere:** buttons use tvOS 26's glass styles (`.glass`, and `.glassProminent` in
  the teal accent for the main action) and floating panels use `glassEffect`. Shared helpers in
  `Theme.swift` (`arcGlassButton`, `arcGlassPanel`, `roundIconButton`) fall back to bordered
  buttons and thin material on tvOS 18–25.
- **Home:** a full-screen hero for the focused title (its background, logo or name, details,
  overview) with a glass Play capsule (showing progress and minutes left when part-watched) and
  round Watchlist and More Info buttons. The rows sit beneath with the first peeking up; the
  profile button row at the top is gone (the sidebar and Settings have Switch Profile).
- **Cards:** smaller, rounder posters and stills with the title and year (or episode code and
  time left) underneath, a white progress capsule and a corner tick for watched.
- **Library:** the name, then Titles / Collections / Folders as pills in one glass capsule, with
  the count and Sort and Filter capsules on the right (as the Android library header).
- **Title page:** a glass Play / Resume capsule followed by round icon-only glass buttons
  (From Beginning, Watched, Watchlist), as the Android title page; seasons are glass pills.
- **Player:** the title large at the bottom left (episode code and show above it), a thin white
  timeline with a knob, times beneath and a softer shade. Subtitles and Audio open in a glass
  panel down the right-hand side, a vertical list per section, so the picture stays visible.
  Skip Intro, Up Next and the error pane use glass buttons.

## Decisions

- Differs from Plex for Apple TV (which keeps a banner-less Home and top-left title): the owner
  chose the reference / Android look. Logged on arc-media-server#80.
- Icon-only buttons keep their names as accessibility labels, so VoiceOver and the UI test
  still find them.

## Logging

No change: these are views over the same calls, which already log their failures.

## Limits

Glass is drawn only on tvOS 26; older systems get the plain fallbacks. Checked in the simulator
walk-through only; not yet on a real Apple TV.
