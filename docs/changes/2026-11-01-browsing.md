# Browsing: tabs, libraries, search, Settings and title actions

## What changed

- **Tabs** along the top (tvOS's own tab bar, standing in for Plex's sidebar sources): Search,
  Home, the Watchlist and each library in the profile's order (Arrange libraries on the web,
  `library_order` and `watchlist_position`), Playlists and Settings. Each tab keeps its own
  pages, so Back goes back up the tab you're in, then to the tabs.
- **Libraries**: a poster grid of every title, 60 at a time as you scroll, with the web's sorts
  (Title, Date Added, Release Date, Year, Rating, Duration, Last Watched; choosing the sort again
  reverses it) and filters (Unwatched Only, genre, decade, and resolution for movies), from
  `/libraries/{id}/facets`. Titles / Collections switch the grid to the library's collections;
  Other videos libraries add Folders.
- **Pages**: collections, playlists, people (their titles in your libraries) and folders.
- **Search**: the tvOS search keyboard; results follow the typing after a short pause, in rows of
  movies, shows, episodes, collections and people.
- **Title actions**: holding Select on any card opens Play/Resume, Mark as Watched/Unwatched,
  Add to/Remove from Watchlist and Go to Show (episodes). The title page has the watched and
  Watchlist buttons next to Play.
- **Title page rows**: Cast & Crew (opens the person), Collections and More Like This.
- **Settings**: Auto-Play, Skip Intros Automatically and the subtitle mode, saved to the
  profile's preferences on Arc (so they follow the profile to every app), plus Switch Profile,
  Sign Out (confirmed), Use Another Server, the version and TMDB/TheTVDB attribution.
- Home, title pages and grids read again after playing or a change of watch state.
- ArcKit: the API calls and models for all of this, with tests (`BrowseTests`). The stand-in
  Arc records and answers the new endpoints, and the walk-through screenshots the new screens.

## Decisions

- Tabs rather than a sidebar: tvOS 17 (the app's minimum) has no sidebar tab view, and the top
  tab bar is what tvOS apps use. Plex's Apple TV app uses a sidebar; this can follow when the
  minimum moves to tvOS 18 (`.sidebarAdaptable`). Logged on arc-media-server#80.
- Playlists is always a tab, with a hint when there are none, so it's where people expect it.
- Settings shows only what applies to watching on a TV; everything else stays on the web.

## Limits

- Not yet: playing a whole collection or playlist (play queues), editing playlists, the
  subtitle language and style, extras.
- Checked in the simulator against the stand-in only; not on a real Apple TV.

## Logging

Page loads log a warning once when they fail (with the page and error type, no addresses), a
library page's load time at debug, each preference change at info and failures of title actions
as warnings. Nothing logs tokens or addresses.
