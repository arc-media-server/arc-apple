# Batch 4: Hide Spoilers, queue editing, live updates, More Like This

Built on batch 3 (#26), one commit per change.

## Hide Spoilers

arc-media-server#476 listed Hide Spoilers as done on Apple TV, but the app had no code for it. It
now works as on the web and Android: a **Hide Spoilers** switch in Settings (the profile's
`hide_spoilers` preference, so it's the same setting as everywhere else), and while it's on, an
episode the profile hasn't watched or started shows the show's background in place of its still
(the still blurred when the show has none) and "Description hidden until you watch it." in place
of its description. This covers Home's cards and hero, search results, a season's episodes and
the title page (header and About).

**Decisions:** the switch sits with the other playback switches in Settings rather than in a
section of its own. The player's info panel still shows the description: you're already watching
it. As Android, a Home hero background is the show's background where it has one.

## Queue row editing

Holding Select on a card in Home's Queue row now offers **Move Earlier**, **Move Later** and
**Remove from Queue** for the titles after the one playing (Arc refuses to remove the one
playing, and nothing moves in front of it), with Android's names and rules. ArcKit's
`PlayQueues` gains `remove` and `move` (Arc's `remove` and `reorder` edits). If the queue changed
somewhere else, they read it again and try once more, like the other queue calls.

## Live updates

The app now keeps Arc's events stream (`GET /api/v1/events`) open while a profile's pages are on
screen, copied from Android's version (which follows the web's):

- Home's rows, the sidebar's libraries, a title's page, Watch Together's rooms and Hide Spoilers
  reload in place when Arc says something they show changed.
- Hints are gathered: the first after 1.5 seconds, then at most one refresh every 5 seconds, so
  a library scan doesn't reload Home constantly.
- The player is never reloaded under the viewer (pages read again after playing anyway). Library
  grids and search aren't reloaded while being used: they're current when opened again. A title
  page stays on the season being looked at.
- In the background the stream closes. On return it resumes from where it was (`Last-Event-ID`),
  and after a gap everything refreshes once. If Arc refuses (all its streams in use), the app
  respects its Retry-After, otherwise it waits 5 seconds, doubling up to a minute.
- Home's half-minute check for Watch Together rooms is gone: rooms now follow their own hint.

This follows the current rules, without waiting for arc-media-server#483.

## More Like This

The title page's **Related** row is now **More Like This**, as Plex calls it. This was the owner's
decision on arc-media-server#480, on 19 November. The other apps follow separately.

## Logging

- Live updates log one warning when the stream drops or Arc refuses it, then debug lines until it
  reconnects. Hints that arrive are logged at debug, by topic name only. The stream's address and
  the token are never logged.
- Queue edits that fail log one warning, naming the step. A successful edit logs at debug.
- Hide Spoilers logs nothing new: changing the setting is logged like any other preference.
